How to Play

  • Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries.
  • Each guess must be a valid word. Press Enter to submit.
  • After each guess, tile colors show how close you are: Green correct letter, correct spot Yellow correct letter, wrong spot Gray letter not in the word
  • Your win streak is shown at the top — consecutive wins in a row.
  • Lose a game and your streak resets to zero. Beat your best streak!
